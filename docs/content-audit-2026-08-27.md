## Intentional Exclusions

- `DoroboGO` and deprecated project priority are not restored.
- Simulated latency, traffic, uptime and other unverified metrics are not restored.
- The former computer-vision-first positioning is not restored.
- Repeated technology definitions and decorative dashboard status indicators remain removed.
- No synthetic screenshots or project claims are introduced.

## Restoration Priorities

1. Restore project evidence and implementation scope on Home.
2. Prefer real screenshots for projects that have product or validation imagery.
3. Replace repeated oversized section spacing with content-led rhythm.
4. Preserve the current LLM, Computer Vision, Resume and Project Detail information architecture.
5. Keep mobile content available; change layout rather than hiding core evidence.
